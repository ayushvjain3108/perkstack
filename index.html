Frequently asked questions

What is PerkStack?

An independent directory of referral links shared by a user of the listed services. It is not an official provider website.

Do I receive a bonus for using a referral link?

It depends on the program. Some links offer a direct bonus; others lead to card welcome offers. Check the linked provider's current offer and requirements.

Does the site owner receive a referral reward?

Yes, potentially. The owner's reward is disclosed on each offer card, and only qualifying referrals generate rewards.

No. The Google Pixel referral promotion ended November 19, 2026, and expired codes are not displayed as working offers.
